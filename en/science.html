6th Gen Industrial 4.0 Low-Temp Biomimetic Extraction

Anthocyanins are highly sensitive to temperature. Traditional high-temperature extraction (60°C-80°C) easily causes polyphenol degradation. The biomimetic technology developed by Prof. Wang Zhenyu's team at HIT strictly controls the temperature below 45°C, effectively simulating the natural biochemical environment within plants.
